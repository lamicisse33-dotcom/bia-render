Model input: mono 16000 Hz PCM [batch, 1, samples]; output: 192-dimensional voice embedding.
The BIA integration compares a temporary session reference locally. No speaker
embedding is persisted or sent to Supabase. This is not authentication and does
not separate overlapping speakers or establish whom somebody is addressing.
